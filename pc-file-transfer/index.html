How it works

Three steps. No account required.

01

Download & run

Open the Companion on your Windows or Linux computer.

02

Scan the QR code

Use the Android app to scan the Companion QR code, then approve the connection.

03

Transfer files

Select a file on either device and send it directly across your local network.

Features

Everything you need. Nothing in the way.

↔ Two-way transfers

Android to desktop and desktop to Android.

⌁ Local network

Works over your local Wi-Fi / LAN without cloud storage.

◇ QR pairing
