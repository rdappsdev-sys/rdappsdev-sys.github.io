PC Media Remote – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

PC Media Remote is developed by RD Apps. This Privacy Policy explains how PC Media Remote handles information when you use the Android application and the PC Media Remote Windows Companion.

Local Network Communication

PC Media Remote communicates with the Windows Companion directly over your local network. This communication is used to discover and connect to your PC, display media information, control media playback, and control supported Windows audio functions.

Remote connections are protected using authenticated pairing and encrypted HTTPS communication. Pairing information and security credentials are stored locally on your devices.

Media Information

The application may process media information provided by supported media players on your Windows PC, such as track or video title, artist, artwork, playback status, playback position, and duration.

This information is transmitted between the Windows Companion and the Android application over your local network for the purpose of providing remote media controls. RD Apps does not operate a server that collects or stores this media information.

Device and Connection Information

PC Media Remote may locally store information required to recognize and securely reconnect to a paired computer, such as device identifiers, connection information, authentication credentials, and certificate information.

This information is used for the operation and security of the connection between your devices.

YouTube Features

Some optional features may interact with YouTube services. Where a feature requires access to a user's Google or YouTube account, authorization is performed through Google's official authentication mechanisms and is subject to Google's applicable privacy policies and terms.

PC Media Remote does not request your Google account password.

Advertising

The current version of PC Media Remote does not contain advertising or an advertising SDK.

If advertising is introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe the advertising services used and any associated data handling before or when those services are introduced.

Data Sharing

RD Apps does not sell personal information collected through PC Media Remote. The core remote-control functionality is designed to operate directly between your Android device and your Windows PC on the local network.

Data Retention and Deletion

Connection and pairing information used by PC Media Remote is stored locally on your devices. Users can remove paired connection information using the available application controls or by uninstalling the application.

Children's Privacy

PC Media Remote is a general-purpose utility and is not specifically directed at children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when PC Media Remote gains new features or when its data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact

For privacy questions or support regarding PC Media Remote, contact RD Apps using the developer contact information provided on the application's Google Play listing.
